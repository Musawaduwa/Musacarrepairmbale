Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Musa Car Wiring & Diagnosis Mbale ("we", "us") runs the website musacarwiring.autos and an auto-electrical and diagnosis workshop on Maluku Road, Mbale City, Uganda. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and what choices you have. It is written with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 in mind.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, car make and model, preferred date and a description of the problem when you use the booking form, WhatsApp, a phone call or email.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and time spent, collected through cookies and similar technologies, including those of Google.

2. The booking form

Our booking form does not save your details on our website. When you press the button it opens WhatsApp with a message containing what you typed, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and has its own privacy terms.

Customer accounts, comments and photos

If you create a customer account, we collect your name, email address and password (the password is handled securely by our account provider and we cannot read it). If you post a comment we also store your comment text, optional car details, rating, any photos you upload, and the likes and replies you post. Comments appear on our website straight after you post them, and we may remove content that breaks our rules. Your comments, photos and the name you entered are shown publicly on our website. Do not include phone numbers, addresses or other personal details in comments or photos.

This data is stored with our cloud provider Supabase, which may process data outside Uganda. We keep your account and comments until you ask us to delete them. To delete your account, comments or photos, email mama1212food@gmail.com or delete your comments yourself in the Customer Panel.

If you are logged in when you use the booking form, we also save your booking details (name, phone number, car, service, preferred date and notes) so you can see them in your dashboard and so we can manage your booking. Questions you post on Ask the Mechanic, with any photos, and the answers and replies are shown publicly, so do not include private information. You can delete your own questions and cancel your own bookings in your dashboard, or email us to delete your data.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to you, give quotes, book and carry out repairs.
  • To keep a record of work done on your vehicle.
  • To keep the site secure and understand which pages are useful.
  • To show advertising (see below).

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and Google advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and read how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. We do not control third-party cookies. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies; the site still works without them, but ads may be less relevant.

5. Other third parties

The site may load fonts, scripts and images from services such as Google Fonts and content delivery networks, and may link to WhatsApp, YouTube, Google Drive and other sites. Those services receive your IP address when they load and have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for them.

6. Storing and sending data outside Uganda

Services we use (such as Google and WhatsApp) may process data in other countries. By using the site and contacting us through these services you agree to this transfer, which is carried out under those providers' own safeguards.

7. How long we keep it

We keep customer contact and vehicle records for as long as needed to give service and warranty support, then delete or anonymise them. Messages on WhatsApp stay until deleted by us.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, such as limiting who can see customer messages. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act you may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, ask us to delete it, object to how it is used, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact us using the details below. If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) at NITA-Uganda.

10. Children

Our site and services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent. If you believe a child gave us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.
